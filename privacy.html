Privacy Policy — 1stTab

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Version 1.0

📌 Short version: 1stTab stores all your data locally in your browser. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. No account required.

1. What data 1stTab stores

All data is saved exclusively in your browser's localStorage on your device. It never leaves your device to our servers (we have no servers).

DataStorage keyPurpose
Bookmarks and foldersdash.bookmarks.v2, dash.folders.v2Your saved links
Time zone preferencesdash.zones.v1Clock panel configuration
Display preferencesdash.prefs.v1Name, weather city, theme, units
Theme settingsdash.tweaksMaterial/Arcade mode, sub-theme
Background uploadsdash.bgUploads.v1Images you upload as backgrounds
Grid/list viewdash.viewLast-used bookmark layout

You can clear all this data at any time by clearing site data for the extension in Chrome settings, or by using the "Export → Replace" flow to replace it with an empty JSON file.

3. Uploaded background images

Images you upload as custom backgrounds are stored only in your browser's localStorage on your device. They are never uploaded to any server. Images are compressed to JPEG format before storage to reduce size.

4. Search queries

When you use the search bar, your query is sent directly to Google Search (google.com) in a new tab, using the standard Google search URL. This is identical to typing in Chrome's address bar. 1stTab does not intercept, log, or proxy this request.

5. No analytics or tracking

1stTab contains no analytics, tracking pixels, crash reporters, or telemetry of any kind. We do not know how many people use the extension, which features they use, or anything else about their behaviour.

6. Permissions

1stTab does not request any Chrome permissions. It accesses only localStorage, which is available to all extension pages without a permission declaration.

7. Children's privacy

1stTab does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13. As all data is stored locally, there is no server-side data collection of any kind.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and increment the extension version. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Open an issue on our GitHub repository or contact us at the email listed in the Chrome Web Store listing.
