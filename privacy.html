1stTab Privacy Policy

Version 2.2.1

Data processed

1stTab reads and edits your Chrome bookmarks when you use its library. It reads the current page title and URL only following a toolbar action. Bookmark notes, tags, favorites, preferences, migration snapshots and recovery backups are stored in chrome.storage.local on your device.

Sharing

Ordinary local use does not upload your bookmarks or collect analytics. If you explicitly connect Google Drive and start a backup, Google receives the bookmark export described below. Chrome may sync native bookmarks according to your browser settings; extension notes and tags do not automatically follow that sync. Opening a bookmarked URL connects to its website under that website’s own policies. Starter sites use bundled high-resolution icons. Other bookmark icons request a higher-resolution favicon from Chrome’s saved icons. If no saved icon is available, loading an icon requests /favicon.ico from that website without sending a referrer, bookmark notes, or tags. If loading fails, a first letter is shown.

Your controls

You may edit/delete bookmarks, export a copy, and import an export. Exported files contain saved URLs and notes: choose where to store and share them. Uninstalling removes extension storage but leaves native Chrome bookmarks. Migration preserves legacy metadata locally for recovery. Extension backups do not contain API keys or authentication tokens. Google Drive backups remain until you choose Delete backup; disconnecting does not delete backups already stored in Drive.

Permissions

bookmarks manages the native library; storage keeps local metadata and recovery data; sidePanel displays bookmarks while browsing; activeTab reads the current page after your toolbar click; favicon reads Chrome’s saved website icons for bookmark cards and list rows. Google identity and https://www.googleapis.com/* access are optional and requested only when you choose to connect Google Drive. No required external host access, history or hardware permissions.

Optional Google Drive backup

When you connect and start a backup, Google receives a versioned export containing bookmarks, folders, notes, tags, favorites and supported appearance/view preferences in the app-private Drive appDataFolder. 1stTab requests only the drive.appdata scope; it does not request Gmail, Calendar or general Drive-file access. The Drive account identifier and display email/name are used to verify the connection and stored locally with operation status and timestamps; these fields are excluded from exports and cleared on disconnect. Chrome’s identity API handles access tokens; tokens are not logged or exported. Uploads are user-started, and restore is additive with a preview that can show duplicates. Disconnect clears local connection metadata and Chrome cached tokens and removes the extension permission, but does not delete Drive backups. You may delete backups in 1stTab or revoke access through Google account settings. Google processing follows Google’s privacy policy.

Support

Report privacy questions through the project issue tracker. Do not post private bookmarks in public reports.
