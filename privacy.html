1stTab Privacy Policy

Version 1.1.1 · October 6, 2026

Data processed

1stTab reads and edits your Chrome bookmarks when you use its library. It reads the current page title and URL only following a toolbar action. Bookmark notes, tags, favorites, preferences, migration snapshots and recovery backups are stored in chrome.storage.local on your device.

Sharing

This local release does not upload your bookmarks, collect analytics, or send data to AI or cloud providers. Chrome may sync native bookmarks according to your browser settings; extension notes and tags do not automatically follow that sync. Opening a bookmarked URL connects to its website under that website’s own policies.

Your controls

You may edit/delete bookmarks, export a copy, and import an export. Exported files contain saved URLs and notes: choose where to store and share them. Uninstalling removes extension storage but leaves native Chrome bookmarks. Migration preserves legacy metadata locally for recovery. Extension backups do not contain API keys or authentication tokens.

Permissions

bookmarks manages the native library; storage keeps local metadata and recovery data; sidePanel displays bookmarks while browsing; activeTab reads the current page after your toolbar click. No required external host access, history or hardware permissions.

Support

Report privacy questions through the project issue tracker. Do not post private bookmarks in public reports.
