1stTab Complete User Manual

Welcome to 1stTab. This comprehensive manual will guide you through all the features, configurations, and powerful tools built into your new-tab dashboard.

1. The Dashboard Basics

1stTab replaces your default Chrome new tab page with a customizable dashboard. It consists of multiple widgets that you can enable, disable, and rearrange.

  • Arranging Widgets: Simply drag and drop widgets between the left and right columns to organize your layout.
  • Enabling/Disabling Widgets: Open the Settings menu (via the gear icon) and go to the "Widgets" pane to toggle which widgets appear on your dashboard.

2. Keyboard Shortcuts

Navigate and command 1stTab quickly using your keyboard:

  • Alt + Shift + P — Open the 1stTab Side Panel from anywhere in Chrome.
  • ⌘K or Ctrl + K — Focus the global search bar on the dashboard.
  • / — Focus the bookmark filter inside the dashboard.

3. Available Widgets

  • Bookmarks: Quick access to your favorite sites. Supports searching and organizing.
  • Notes (Scratchpad): A handy text area for quick thoughts, links, or reminders. Saved locally automatically.
  • To-Do (Today): A daily task manager to keep track of what needs doing.
  • Time & World Clocks: Keep track of different time zones. Plus users get access to premium analog clock faces.
  • Weather: Local weather updates right on your new tab.
  • Finance & Quotes: Daily quotes and finance trackers to keep you inspired and informed.

4. Bookmarks Manager

Open the Bookmarks Manager to access advanced tools for organizing your saved links:

  • Tree View: An intuitive tree structure for navigating folders.
  • Drag and Drop: Easily move bookmarks and entire folders around to reorganize your collection.
  • Create & Edit: Add new folders and edit bookmark URLs or titles seamlessly.

5. AI Bookmark Assistant

1stTab features an intelligent AI assistant to help you manage and find bookmarks. To enable it:

  1. Navigate to the "AI & API Keys" section in Settings.
  2. Select your preferred AI provider: OpenAI ChatGPT, Anthropic Claude, or Google Gemini.
  3. Enter your personal API key (obtained from your provider's developer platform).

Once activated, the AI Assistant enables intelligent categorization and conversational chat interactions directly with your bookmarks.

6. Themes and Appearance

Personalize your 1stTab experience with themes:

  • Standard Themes: Switch between clean Light and Dark modes.
  • Arcade Mode: Turn your dashboard into a retro experience featuring variants like Pac-Man and Space Invaders.
  • Custom CSS: Plus users have access to unlimited custom CSS to tweak the dashboard exactly to their liking.

7. Backup and Sync

Never lose your data. 1stTab allows you to back up your settings and bookmarks. Go to Settings to manually export and import your configuration. Plus users can also link their Google Drive account for automated cloud backups and cross-device sync.
