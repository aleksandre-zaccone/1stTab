1stTab Privacy Policy

Optional release candidate 1.2.1 · October 6, 2026

Data processed

1stTab reads and edits your Chrome bookmarks when you use its library. It reads the current page title and URL only following a toolbar action. Bookmark notes, tags, favorites, preferences, migration snapshots and recovery backups are stored in chrome.storage.local on your device.

Sharing

Ordinary local operations do not upload your bookmarks or collect analytics. Optional AI and Google Drive operations send the data described below only after you explicitly grant access and start the operation. Chrome may sync native bookmarks according to your browser settings; extension notes and tags do not automatically follow that sync. Opening a bookmarked URL connects to its website under that website’s own policies.

Your controls

You may edit/delete bookmarks, export a copy, and import an export. Exported files contain saved URLs and notes: choose where to store and share them. Uninstalling removes extension storage but leaves native Chrome bookmarks. Migration preserves legacy metadata locally for recovery. Extension backups do not contain API keys or authentication tokens.

Permissions

bookmarks manages the native library; storage keeps local metadata and recovery data; sidePanel displays bookmarks while browsing; activeTab reads the current page after your toolbar click. No required external host access, history or hardware permissions. Optional identity permission and provider-specific host access are requested when enabling Google Drive or AI.

Support

Report privacy questions through the project issue tracker. Do not post private bookmarks in public reports.

Optional AI organization

If you explicitly request suggestions, OpenAI receives selected bookmark IDs, titles and URLs, and writable folder IDs/names. If you choose to find within selected links, your search query is also sent. Bookmark notes and unselected links are excluded. Your API key authorizes the request, stays in the dialog and is not saved or backed up. Requests can incur provider costs; processing and retention follow your provider account and OpenAI’s policy. Suggestions require a review before any bookmark edits. Disable AI access removes provider host permission.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you connect and upload, Google receives the bookmark export including notes and tags in the app-private Drive appDataFolder. Only drive.appdata is requested; no Calendar, Tasks or email access. Tokens are handled by Chrome’s identity API and are not logged or exported. There is no automatic upload. Backups remain until you choose Delete backup. Restores require an additive import preview and can create duplicates. Disconnect clears Chrome cached tokens and removes extension permission; you may also revoke the app through Google account settings. Google processing follows Google’s privacy policy.
